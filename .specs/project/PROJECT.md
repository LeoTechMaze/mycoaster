# PROJECT.md — MyCoaster

## Vision
A casa digital do parqueiro — o app definitivo para entusiastas de parques de diversão e montanhas-russas rastrearem coasters andados, avaliarem experiências, descobrirem novos parques, e se conectarem com a comunidade.

## Positioning
Mobile-first social platform for coaster enthusiasts. Tracking + structured reviews + community gallery + leaderboard competition. Brazilian market first, global expansion horizon.

## Target Audience
- Enthusiasts who want to track every coaster they've ridden
- Content creators (YouTube/Instagram/TikTok) in the theme park niche
- Casual visitors who want to plan trips and read community reviews
- Primary market: Brazil; secondary: international Portuguese speakers → global

## Platforms
- iOS and Android (React Native, no Expo)

## Core Value Props
1. **Tracking** — mark coasters as ridden, accumulate credits, earn badge levels
2. **Reviews** — rate parks and coasters 1–5 stars with free-text comments
3. **Discovery** — find parks and coasters by GPS proximity or city/country search
4. **Community** — gallery of community photos, YouTube video links, leaderboard
5. **AI Intelligence** — AI-generated review summaries + clickable thematic tags (Phase 4)

## Out of Scope (all phases)
- Coaster technical data (height, speed, length)
- Friend activity feed / timeline
- Push notifications
- Offline mode
- Video storage (YouTube URLs only)
- Weighted ratings (all users equal weight)
- Fixed review subcategories (replaced by AI-emergent tags)
- Coaster filtering by type, height, speed

## Success Metrics (to define)
- User registrations and DAU/MAU
- Credits logged per user (engagement depth)
- Reviews written per user
- Photos uploaded and liked
- Leaderboard participation rate
